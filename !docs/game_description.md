# Echoes of the Sol Frontier

This is a sandbox, 2D tile-based real-time game set in a realistic near-future space environment. The game has no fixed objectives, allowing players the freedom to immerse themselves in the joy of role-playing and base-building, crafting their own unique space story. The player controls a single avatar, with the camera constantly following their movements as they navigate their space base.

## Base Construction & Crafting
Buildings and walls align with tiles, while objects and avatars can move freely within this grid. The world is fully interactive, with every item crafted using a 3D printer and assembled or disassembled using the appropriate tools. Resources needed for crafting can be mined from asteroids, purchased, or even gathered from space (e.g., extracting water from meteorites).

## Player Progression & Crew Management
The player starts with no specialization, gaining experience by performing tasks just like any other NPC. As the player progresses, they develop skills that help in various roles, from engineering to research. Crew management is streamlined; AI-controlled crew members are autonomous and capable of handling routine tasks like janitoring without the need for micromanagement. Crew members develop relationships, such as friendships, rivalries, or even romances, but these interactions are grounded in realism—don’t expect highly qualified scientists to brawl over a disagreement on day one.

## Environmental Simulation
The environment is highly simulated, adding a layer of realism to gameplay. Removing a wall can lead to air loss, cold temperatures cause water to freeze, and breaking a water pump results in leaks. External factors like solar flares may disrupt communications and solar panel functionality, requiring players to plan and adapt their base designs accordingly.

## Economic and Scientific Progress
Players can start on Earth, represented by a single area— a space center where they can walk, perform tasks, and interact with the environment much like in space. From here, they can look for jobs, hire shuttles to orbit or further into space, order material deliveries, and prepare for their space endeavors. As players venture into space, bases and objects are procedurally generated, ensuring each playthrough feels unique.

## Exploration & Expeditions
Players can build a port for a utility shuttle, allowing them to send expeditions for various purposes: landing on Earth, mining asteroids, trading with other bases, and more. These expeditions are key to gathering resources and expanding the player's base of operations.

## Resource Management & Power Generation
Electricity is generated through various means, including solar panels, fuel generators, and more powerful but dangerous options like nuclear reactors or antimatter void generators. Players must manage these resources carefully to keep their base operational.

## Trading & Economy
Trading is straightforward, with each good having a buy and sell price based on supply and demand. Players can trade with other bases, planets, or even smugglers. The economy operates autonomously, meaning it functions and evolves independently of the player's actions, providing a dynamic backdrop to the gameplay.

## Atmosphere & Music
The game's atmosphere evokes a sense of space loneliness, with calm, ambient music enhancing the solitary experience. It's a slow-paced, meditative game that focuses on the satisfaction of building and maintaining a space base, with logical consequences for player decisions.

## Future Expansions
Terraforming and interactions with aliens are exciting possibilities for the future but will be introduced as DLC, expanding the gameplay and offering new challenges and experiences.
